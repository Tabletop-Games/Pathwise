# Unified Store design

The current application has no custom backend. Coin debits and ownership changes are recorded together in the existing local versioned snapshot; stable `tip/<id>` ledger identifiers reject duplicate charges. Play Games Saved Games synchronizes the economy as one unit and asks the player to resolve conflicting economy copies. The v2 cloud envelope reads v1 payloads after validating their original checksums, then adds Classic ownership and selection. Existing Play Billing and AdMob callbacks retain their own verification and deduplication paths.

A fixed Store header and category selector surround lazy Themes, Tips, Coins and Upgrades catalogues. Theme thumbnails share a valid partial route from the actual Level 1 board; candidate palettes are isolated from the equipped Store theme. A shared vector painter supplies catalogue, larger preview and gameplay artwork. Tip orientation comes from accepted route edges and skips portal transport. Each paid tip has its own entry in the economy catalogue.

Automatic coin-video offers are considered only after a completed level is exited. Both a five-new-level threshold and a fifteen-minute interval apply; cooldown data is persisted. Existing foreground, connectivity, entitlement, video readiness, daily reward and ad-spacing checks remain in force. Earned rewards continue through the existing rewarded callback.
