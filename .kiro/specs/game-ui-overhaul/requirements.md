# Requirements: game-first UI/UX overhaul

Android only. No hosted backend. Economy, puzzle rules, prices, odds, limits, saves and purchases are unchanged.

1. **Show, don't explain.** Screens communicate with original vector art, counters, progress indicators and short labels. Long paragraphs, repeated facts and debug-like text are removed. Essential disclosures (purchase terms, ad disclosure, privacy, account recovery, destructive actions) stay reachable.
2. **One design system.** Typography, spacing, radii, price pills, action pills, segmented selector, cards, dialogs, progress pips and reward displays come from `lib/ui/game_ui.dart` and resolve every colour from the equipped `BoardPalette`.
3. **Main menu.** A full-width Store card follows Time Attack. The header Store bag and the standalone Themes tile are removed. The coin balance still opens Coins. Fortune Wheel is a compact full-width card. Research and Boosts stay a two-column row. The developer dashboard stays debug-only.
4. **Store.** Fixed header with balance. Full-width four-segment selector (Themes, Tips, Coins, Upgrades). Flat theme-derived cards. Locked items show an accent price pill with the coin icon and a number only; owned items show a quiet Apply; the equipped item shows a subdued Equipped state.
5. **Themes grid.** Three columns on portrait phones. Each card has a miniature Level 1 board using the candidate theme, the name and one action. The surrounding UI uses the equipped theme. Tapping the thumbnail opens a larger preview.
6. **Tips.** All 19 paid tips have finished original layered vector artwork, upright in the catalogue and rotated by cardinal facing in play. IDs, ownership and the 100-coin prices do not change. Artwork resolves colours from the equipped theme with contrast-aware transforms. No emoji, glyphs or Material icons as artwork.
7. **Coins.** A reward card with coin art, the reward amount, a Watch action, the daily counter and a four-stage milestone indicator. When the next eligible video also earns the milestone bonus, the card shows the combined total and its breakdown. Ad states: ready, loading, unavailable, daily limit, milestone available, milestone claimed, processing. Coin packs show differentiated stacks, quantity and the Play-localised price. When billing is unavailable the packs stay visible with a concise unavailable state. No invented prices, discounts or claims.
8. **Upgrades.** Remove Ads, Themes Forever and Premium have distinct illustrations, compact benefit chips, the Play price and an owned state. Entitlements, bonuses and purchase management stay as they are.
9. **Gameplay.** The board is the focus. Permanent instruction text is removed in favour of onboarding and a Help control. Header, progress, controls and completion are tightened.
10. **Continuous cardinal pointer tracking.** During a drag the tip follows the finger continuously along a horizontal or vertical axis through the last accepted cell. The leading path segment meets the tip. Direction uses dominant-axis selection with hysteresis and a dead zone. The tip never faces or draws diagonally. Logical route state only changes through the existing validator. Walls, gates, wrong checkpoints and visited tiles clamp the tip before the obstacle. Ice slides and portals rebase tracking without a jump. Release or cancel settles the tip on the accepted cell. Nothing transient is persisted, synced or sent to economy or ad code.
11. **Checkpoint tips.** The tip keeps one size on every cell. On a checkpoint it sits on the leading edge of the checkpoint disc and the number stays readable.
12. **Other screens.** Levels, Daily, Time Attack, Fortune Wheel, Research, Boosts, crates, achievements, statistics, streaks, tutorials, completion, reward and insufficient-balance dialogs, errors, loading, connection loss, cloud save and Settings follow the design system and the text-density rules.
13. **Accessibility.** Touch targets of at least 48 dp, descriptive semantics labels for compact visuals, reduced-motion support, contrast checks per theme, large-text layouts without overflow.
14. **Verification.** Format, analyze, unit and widget tests, representative theme and viewport captures that are actually inspected, economy simulation, Android debug APK, minified release APK and AAB, R8 checks, CI.
