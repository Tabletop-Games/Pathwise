# Game modes

| Mode | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Levels | Solve progressively harder unlimited boards. Revisit cleared levels for a better time. |
| Daily Challenge | Solve three deterministic boards for the local calendar day. Existing single-board saves remain valid. |
| Time Attack | Solve as many boards as possible in one, three, or five minutes. The timer uses boot-relative elapsed time through backgrounding. Stop and Restart discard the active run; records are separate for each duration. |

All three work offline and share the core route rules. Levels introduce advanced mechanics, and Daily Challenge and Time Attack keep classic movement. Eligible Levels and Daily Challenge play earn supply crates. Share the real challenge date, elapsed time and hints from Daily Challenge, the score and solve count from Time Attack, and numbered level milestones from Levels. [Gameplay](gameplay.md) · [Sharing](social-sharing.md)


New Time Attack runs introduce guided, sparse, and standard checkpoint layouts after the first three solves. The generator keeps any extra clue necessary to certify a unique full route. Restored legacy runs keep their original puzzle seeds. The timer continues during voluntarily opened video ads and app backgrounding.
