# Play Games

The Android bridge uses Play Games Services v2 for sign-in, native achievement and leaderboard views, and Saved Games for cloud progress (see [cloud saves](cloud-saves.md)). Sign-in is optional. Achievement eligibility is recorded on the device and synchronized when Play Games is available; gameplay does not need it. Configured leaderboards open in native read-only views. Authoritative ranked submissions, score proofs and Play Integrity are not available.

See [Google Play achievements](google-play-achievements.md) for the achievement catalogue, synchronization rules and Console setup, and [play-console-setup.md](play-console-setup.md#3-play-games-services-project-play-console-owner) for the publisher checklist.
