# Play Games

The Android bridge uses Play Games Services v2 for sign-in, native achievement and leaderboard views, and Saved Games for cloud progress (see [cloud saves](cloud-saves.md)). Gameplay needs an internet connection. Achievement eligibility is recorded on the device and synchronized when Play Games is available.

See [Google Play Games achievements](google-play-achievements.md) for the baseline audit, complete catalog, synchronization when Play Games is available, Android verification and Console instructions. [Publisher setup](../docs/play-console-setup.md#3-play-games-services-project-play-console-owner) is the external setup checklist. Configured leaderboards open in native read-only views. Authoritative ranked submissions, score proofs, daily online rankings and Play Integrity remain unavailable.
