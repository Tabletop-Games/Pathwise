Buying adds inventory. Activating consumes one and saves the expiry. There is one coin boost slot. Crate categories can run alongside one another and alongside a coin boost. Same-slot activations cannot extend or stack an active effect. Boost Efficiency extends duration and Smart Savings reduces price. Coin boosts multiply eligible first-level and daily rewards only. The maximum gameplay multiplier is 4×. Purchases, rewarded videos, achievements, streak bonuses and Time Attack scores are outside that multiplier. Monotonic elapsed time governs active effects on Android. Device reboot expires active effects while retaining inventory.
