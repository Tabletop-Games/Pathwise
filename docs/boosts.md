# Boosts

| Effect | Minutes | Coins | Optional video |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2× eligible coins | 10 | 20 | Up to three per day |
| 2× eligible coins | 60 | 100 | — |
| 3× eligible coins | 15 | 60 | One separate video after completing four coin videos that day |
| 4× eligible coins | 10 | 70 | — |

Coin purchases and earned video rewards add items to inventory. Activate them from the Boosts screen. A single coin multiplier can run at a time. Existing versions' Boost inventory stays usable. Research still applies eligible purchase discounts and duration bonuses. Only eligible puzzle-clearing earnings are multiplied. [Economy](economy.md)
