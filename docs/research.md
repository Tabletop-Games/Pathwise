# Research

Auxbrain describes permanent investment decisions and several viable play styles in its description of Egg, Inc. Pathwise uses that progression idea for levels, daily play and supply crates. Each line can improve a different play style. [Egg, Inc. on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/egg-inc/id993492744)

Research ranks are permanent and use the same local coin ledger as cosmetics. The original 13 families use `floor(base × (rank + 2) × (rank + 3) / 6)`, where rank is the currently owned rank.

| Research | Base coins | Max rank | Per-rank effect |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Coin Magnet | 70 | 10 | +2 on a first level clear |
| Golden Route | 100 | 8 | +2 on an unassisted eligible first clear |
| Daily Fortune | 100 | 8 | +4 on a first daily clear |
| Milestone Mastery | 180 | 6 | +5 on newly earned achievements |
| Treasure Hunter | 120 | 8 | 4 seconds between crate opportunities removed |
| Crate Collector | 90 | 9 | Extends the crate flight |
| Boost Efficiency | 160 | 8 | +5% boost duration |
| Smart Savings | 140 | 6 | 3% boost price discount, rounded up after discount |
| Momentum | 150 | 8 | +4 at every fifth first level clear |
| Daily Specialist | 210 | 6 | +8 on each seven-day streak award |

The seven advanced families use `base × [1, 3, 7, 16, 38, 80, 150, 260, 420, 600][owned rank]`. Every specialty requires rank 3 of its prerequisite. The three ten-rank specialties triple the normal increment on ranks 9 and 10. No purchase changes whether a player may play a mode, the hint capacity, or the fourth-hint rule.

| Specialty | Base | Max rank | Effect per ordinary rank | Requires rank 3 |

| Boost Tuning | 190 | 8 | +4 duration percentage points | Boost Efficiency |
| Contract Supply | 160 | 8 | +2 boost discount points | Smart Savings |
| Supply Routes | 200 | 8 | –3 seconds between crate offers | Treasure Hunter |
