# Supply crates

Supply crates appear in a reserved tray during eligible noncompetitive gameplay. They remain outside the grid and cannot be claimed during a route gesture. The tray animation honors the system's reduced-motion setting.

| Rarity | Base odds | Coins |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Common | 75% | 12 |
| Uncommon | 18% | 25 |
| Rare | 6% | 55 |
| Legendary | 1% | 150 |

The first opportunity starts after 45 seconds of eligible play. Later opportunities have a 150-second base interval and a 24-second window. There is one active offer and at most six offers per local day. Research and active boosts can change interval, window, amount and rare odds. Base expected value is 18.30 coins before collection probability. Collection is free. The ledger uses a stable crate ID, and crates contain coins without hint tokens or prizes of monetary value.

Rare Discovery adds up to six rare percentage points. Legendary Discovery adds up to four legendary percentage points. The optional rarity boost adds eight rare points. Each of these transfers odds from common drops. Rare Salvage adds up to 112 coins to rare drops. Legendary Cache adds up to 75 coins to legendary drops. Treasure Hunter and Supply Routes cut the base interval as far as 94 seconds before a frequency boost. The six-offer daily cap is shared across modes. The Wheel's supply cache is a separate 20-coin prize and follows no crate roll.
