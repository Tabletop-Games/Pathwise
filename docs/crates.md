# Flying supply crates

Levels and Daily Challenge offer a crate after 45 seconds of eligible foreground play, then at 150-second base intervals. Time accrues across puzzles. The next offer waits for a break between strokes. A falling crate remains catchable over the completion card until its usual flight ends. Leaving the board forfeits the event. At most eight offers occur per rolling 24 hours.

| 90 | 5% |

The prize is fixed at spawn. The crate falls from a random horizontal position over the board without changing the layout and disappears if missed. Crate Collector extends the eight-second flight. Treasure Hunter, Supply Routes and the supply relay boost shorten the interval between offers. Retired reward modifiers are refunded from recorded purchases where applicable. Old unopened crates are discarded. Eligibility and claim history remain saved.

The server decides when a crate may drop, using its own clock and the eight-per-window cap, and fixes the prize when it drops. A tap opens a reward dialog. Dismissing it forfeits the crate. An unavailable or incomplete video can be retried while the dialog is open. Coins are granted only after the server receives AdMob's signed callback for that crate's reward session. The placement shares the Coins ad unit. In debug builds the puzzle toolbar can drop a test crate with controls to simulate both outcomes.

The expected payout is 40.50 coins for a claimed crate, or 324 coins across eight successful claims.
