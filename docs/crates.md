# Flying supply crates

Levels and Daily Challenge offer a crate after 45 seconds of eligible foreground play, then at 150-second base intervals. Time accrues across puzzles. The next offer waits for a break between strokes. Puzzle completion and backing out forfeit an active event. At most six offers occur per rolling 24 hours.

| Coins | Chance |
| ---: | ---: |
| 25 | 35% |
| 40 | 30% |
| 60 | 20% |
| 85 | 10% |
| 120 | 5% |

The prize is fixed at spawn. The crate falls from a random horizontal position over the board without changing the layout and disappears if missed. Crate Collector extends the eight-second flight. Treasure Hunter, Supply Routes and the supply relay boost shorten the interval between offers. Retired reward modifiers are refunded from recorded purchases where applicable. Old unopened crates are discarded. Eligibility and claim history remain saved.

A tap opens a reward dialog. Dismissing it forfeits the crate. An unavailable or incomplete video can be retried while the dialog is open. The SDK earned callback authorizes the locally recorded coin reward. The crate and callback IDs deduplicate delivery. The placement shares the Coins ad unit. In debug builds the puzzle toolbar can drop a test crate with controls to simulate both outcomes.
