# Pathwise — Economy 2.0 & Offline-First Report

## 1. Executive Summary
Economy 2.0 eliminates the exponential compounding between permanent Research upgrades and timed Coin Boosts, rebalances Boost tiers and Research pricing, introduces rarity-tiered Path Tip pricing, and replaces the cosmetic-only pending discovery queue with a predetermined, single-cycle **Mystery Reward** system.

## 2. Reward Multiplication Rule
- **Campaign First Clear**: `5 × timedMultiplier + magnet + (hintFree ? golden : 0) + (5thClear ? momentum : 0)`
  - Unboosted max research: `5 + 10 + 8 (+16 on 5th) = 23` (or `39` on 5th), down from `49` (`81`).
  - `4×` Boost max research: `20 + 10 + 8 (+16 on 5th) = 38` (or `54` on 5th), down from `196` (`324`).
- **Daily Challenge First Clear**: `10 × timedMultiplier + fortune + (hintFree ? golden : 0)`
  - `4×` Boost max research: `40 + 16 + 8 = 64` (down from `232`).
- **Completion Video Multiplier**: `5 × (multiplier - 1)` (`+5`, `+10`, or `+15` coins), calculated independently of timed boosts and research.

## 4. Research Rebalance & Pricing
- Removed accelerated scaling (`> 8` extra ranks); all 13 branches scale linearly (`owned * increment`).
- Total cost across all 101 ranks reduced from **365,230** coins to **98,585** coins by replacing the extreme advanced curve `[1, 3, 7, 16, 38, 80, 150, 260, 420, 600]` with `[1, 2, 4, 7, 11, 16, 22, 30, 40, 52]`.
- Combined caps: **+24%** boost duration (`16%` Boost Efficiency + `8%` Boost Tuning), **14%** boost discount (`6%` Smart Savings + `8%` Contract Supply), **-40s** crate cooldown (`-24s` Treasure Hunter + `-16s` Supply Routes).
- Full per-rank cost and payback table: [research_payback.csv](reports/research_payback.csv).

## 5. Hints, Freezes & Cosmetics
- **Hint Price**: `40` coins (down from `60`).
- **Streak Freeze Price**: `150` coins (down from `250`, max 2 held).
- **Path Tips** (`6,520` coins total for 19 paid tips): Free (`classic`), Common `120` (6), Uncommon `250` (6), Rare `500` (5), Epic `900` (2).
- **Themes**: `0` to `60,000` coins (`128,900` total across 19 paid themes).

## 6. Mystery Rewards (10-Clear Cycle)
- Predetermined deterministically at cycle start (`selectMysteryReward`); never rerolled on restart, theme change, offline/online transitions, or replay.
- **Category weights**: Coins `40%` (EV `38.50` coins: `25` 50%, `40` 30%, `60` 15%, `100` 5%), Boosts `30%`, Path Tips `20%`, Themes `10%`. Exhausted cosmetic categories redistribute weight deterministically to valid categories.
- **No Pending Queue**: Unclaimed mystery offers are discarded if skipped or abandoned; durable transaction recovery protects genuinely completed ad callbacks.

## 8. Limitations & Notes
- Simulation outcomes model synthetic player behavior and are not live production telemetry.
- Local-first offline saves are client-managed; real-money IAPs, rewarded/interstitial ads, and Play Games cloud sync remain network-dependent.
